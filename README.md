# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

An end-to-end data analytics project designed to uncover actionable consumer insights, optimize marketing strategies, and improve subscription retention using **Python**, **SQL (MySQL)**, and **Power BI**.

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes consumer transactional data across **3,900 purchases** and **18 key features**. The objective is to evaluate demographics, purchase frequencies, product preferences, and promotional impacts to drive strategic business growth.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Workflow
* **Python (Pandas, NumPy):** Used for Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), missing value imputation (median rating by category), feature engineering (`age_group`), and data cleaning.
* **SQL (MySQL):** Integrated clean datasets into a relational database to execute complex business queries (segmentation, revenue breakdowns, and retention metrics).
* **Power BI:** Built an interactive dashboard to visually communicate key trends to stakeholders.

## 📊 Key Analytical Questions Answered (SQL)
* **Demographic Revenue:** Revenue performance compared across genders and binned age groups.
* **Customer Segmentation:** Classification of buyers into *New*, *Returning*, and *Loyal* groups based on historical purchase data.
* **Subscription & Loyalty:** Deep dive into whether high-frequency buyers (>5 purchases) correlate with active subscription sign-ups.
* **Product & Discount Optimization:** Identification of top 5 highest-rated products, top 3 products per category, and highly discount-dependent categories.
* **Logistics Analysis:** Average spend comparison between Standard vs. Express shipping models.

## 🚀 Strategic Business Recommendations
1. **Boost Subscriptions:** Launch target campaigns showing high-value rewards for recurring customers.
2. **Loyalty Infrastructure:** Implement multi-tiered reward structures to transition *Returning* buyers into the highly profitable *Loyal* segment.
3. **Optimized Discount Policy:** Restructure margins on highly discount-dependent products to preserve profitability.
4. **Targeted Campaigns:** Reallocate marketing budget toward high-revenue demographic brackets and express shipping cohorts.
